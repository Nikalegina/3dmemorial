## Source-derived facts used in the application

The source describes artistic laminated safety glass (triplex) built from two glass sheets bonded with polymer. Full-color UV artwork is placed inside the laminate before final assembly.

For full glass steles, the published standard tables use total thicknesses:
- 12 mm;
- 16 mm.

The source also explicitly describes 12 mm as 6+6 mm and 16 mm as 8+8 mm for memorial glass panels.

Published nominal stele panel sizes used as managed presets:
- 400 × 800 mm;
- 400 × 900 mm;
- 450 × 900 mm;
- 500 × 1000 mm;
- 500 × 1100 mm;
- 500 × 1200 mm;
- 600 × 1200 mm.

Published mounting families represented in the editor:
- mounting in a groove;
- point/manet mounting;
- floor glass holders;
- clamp profile.

The source calculator also distinguishes:
- clear M1 glass;
- low-iron/extra-clear glass;
- one-sided versus two-sided UV printing.

Artwork production rule:
- the printed artwork must remain at least 10 mm from the glass-panel edge.

Reference portrait sizes retained for later portrait-product work:
- 130 × 180 mm, 8 mm, non-tempered laminate;
- 180 × 240 mm, 8 mm, non-tempered laminate;
- 240 × 300 mm, 8 mm, non-tempered laminate;
- 300 × 400 mm, tempered laminate;
- 400 × 600 mm, tempered laminate.

Reference glass flowerbed-panel sizes retained for later complex-product work include:
- 1050 × 550 mm;
- 1070 × 600 mm;
- 1070 × 700 mm;
- 1270 × 700 mm;
with 12/16 mm configurations as published.

## What is deliberately NOT imported

- supplier prices;
- supplier warranty promises;
- supplier marketing claims about lifetime or vandal resistance;
- supplier-specific commercial discounts;
- supplier production lead times.

Those are not canonical KRYM MONUMENT product or pricing authorities.

## Product interpretation

The editor treats the published dimensions and construction options as a technical reference for configurable glass memorials, not as a claim that every future KRYM MONUMENT order must be manufactured by this supplier.

Final engineering, hole positions, mounting hardware, foundation/base compatibility and manufacturing tolerances remain subject to project-specific verification.
